# Support

Need a hand with a Wrench-Studio project? Here is how to get help.

## 🐙 For project-specific questions

1. Check the project's **README** for setup, configuration, and usage.
2. Search the **Issues** and **Discussions** tabs on the relevant
   repository — your question may already be answered.
3. If not, open a new **Issue** with:
   - A descriptive title
   - What you tried and what happened
   - Your environment (OS, runtime version, config)

## 🐛 For bugs

See [`.github/CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md#-reporting-bugs) for
what to include in a bug report.

## 🔒 For security issues

**Do not** open a public issue. See
[`.github/SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) for private disclosure.
